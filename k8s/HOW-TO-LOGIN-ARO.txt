================================================================
        HOW TO ACCESS YOUR OPENSHIFT (ARO) LAB
================================================================

Your username    : see the credentials sheet (arosaN)
Your password    : see the credentials sheet
Your project     : you have your own isolated project (namespace)

----------------------------------------------------------------
A) WEB CONSOLE LOGIN  (easiest)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Open in a browser:
     https://console-openshift-console.apps.staragilearo01.southindia.aroapp.io

  2. On the login page, choose the provider:   lab-htpasswd
  3. Enter your username (arosaN) and password.
  4. You land in your own project. You can create Deployments,
     Services, Routes, ConfigMaps, Secrets, PVCs from the UI.

----------------------------------------------------------------
B) COMMAND LINE LOGIN  (oc CLI)
----------------------------------------------------------------
     oc login https://api.staragilearo01.southindia.aroapp.io:6443 -u arosaN -p <password>

     oc get all            (works - shows your project)
     oc new-app https://github.com/<repo>    (build + deploy from Git)

  NOTE: cluster-wide commands (oc get nodes, other projects) will
  say "Forbidden" - this is EXPECTED. You only have your own project.

================================================================
   CONTAINER REGISTRY - push your own images
================================================================
Registry URL:
   default-route-openshift-image-registry.apps.staragilearo01.southindia.aroapp.io

STEP 1 - Log in to OpenShift (see B above), then log in to the registry.
   The registry password is your login TOKEN, not your normal password:

   PowerShell / bash:
     oc whoami -t | docker login -u arosaN --password-stdin ^
        default-route-openshift-image-registry.apps.staragilearo01.southindia.aroapp.io ^
        --tls-verify=false

   (podman works the same - replace 'docker' with 'podman')

STEP 2 - Tag your image for YOUR project, then push:
     docker tag myapp:latest ^
       default-route-openshift-image-registry.apps.staragilearo01.southindia.aroapp.io/arosaN/myapp:latest

     docker push ^
       default-route-openshift-image-registry.apps.staragilearo01.southindia.aroapp.io/arosaN/myapp:latest ^
       --tls-verify=false

STEP 3 - Use the image in a Deployment (reference the INTERNAL path):
     image-registry.openshift-image-registry.svc:5000/arosaN/myapp:latest

IMPORTANT:
  - Replace  arosaN  with YOUR own username / project everywhere.
  - Push URL   = the external 'default-route-...' address (from your laptop).
  - Pod image  = the internal '...svc:5000' address (inside the cluster).
  - You can ONLY push to your own project. Pushing to another
    participant's project is blocked (401 Unauthorized).
  - --tls-verify=false is needed (the route uses a self-signed cert).

  EASIEST (no local docker needed): let OpenShift build it for you-
     oc new-app https://github.com/<your-repo>
  This builds the image inside the cluster and pushes to the registry
  automatically.
